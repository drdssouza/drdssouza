## About Me

I'm an AI Engineer focused on building practical **Generative AI** solutions. I have experience implementing AI across various sectors, including Finance, Insurance, and Education.

Currently, I work at **Compass UOL** as an AI Consultant. My day-to-day involves architecting systems with **Claude and AWS Bedrock**, focusing on process automation and complex data pipelines.
